# Intent: Map with OpenStreetMap

**Owner:** toanlk  **Status:** accepted

## Problem
Xpeet shows how fast I'm going and how far I've driven. It doesn't show where I am or where I went. To see the road around me I have to switch to a maps app, and then the speed is gone. After a drive, I can't look back at the route I took.

I want a map inside Xpeet, next to the speed, using OpenStreetMap. I also want the route of each drive kept on the phone so I can look at it later and delete it when I want.

Every earlier spec kept maps, routes and the network out:
- `specs/speedometer/spec.md`: "No network", "fully offline by design".
- `specs/trip-stats/spec.md`:
  - R9: totals only, no coordinates;
  - Out of scope: "Route, map or position history".
- `specs/speed-accuracy/spec.md` Out of scope: "Map matching or any online data".

The app already reads position and direction of travel for the speed estimate (speed-accuracy R1), so a map needs no new permission.

## Proposed outcome
- **Map mode.** A button on the speed screen switches between the gauge and the map. On the map:
  - a marker shows my position and direction of travel, and the map follows it with no tap;
  - the speed stays on screen as a large readout with its unit, and the status ("Weak GPS signal", "--") still shows;
  - the trip line stays and still opens the trip sheet.
  The app remembers which mode I left it in.
- **Hands-free:** the map zooms to suit the speed (street level in town, wider on the highway). It is heading-up while moving. I can pinch or drag; it returns to following after a few seconds, or with a "re-centre" button.
- **Credit:** "© OpenStreetMap contributors" is always visible on the map.
- **No signal:** tiles I've seen recently still show from the phone's cache. Elsewhere the map is blank, but the marker, trail, speed and trip keep working.
- **Trail:** the map draws the current trip's route as I drive, in one plain colour, never coloured by speed. "New trip" starts a new trail.
- **Route history:** each trip in the trip history (the last 10, trip-stats R8) keeps its route. Tapping an earlier trip in the trip sheet shows its route on a map with its totals.
- **Deleting:**
  - delete one earlier trip together with its route;
  - "Delete all trips" removes every earlier trip and route, plus the current trip's route and totals, after one confirmation. A fresh trip starts.
  - When a trip drops out of the last 10, its route is deleted with it.
- **Measurable:**
  - The marker and the trail move with every GPS fix (about once a second).
  - The speed reading is the same with the map on: speed tests and evals are unchanged, and map data never feeds the speed estimate.
  - A tile is not downloaded again while it is fresh in the cache (the server's cache headers, or at least 7 days).
  - On recorded drives, the length of the stored route is within 2% of the trip distance. Gaps show as breaks in the line, never as straight jumps.
  - One hour of driving stores under 200 KB of route (proposed).
  - Mobile data per hour of driving is measured on a test drive and reported (proposed ceiling: 20 MB/h).

## Affected users & systems
- Users: me (personal use, sideloaded).
- Screens:
  - the speed screen gets a map button and a map mode, in portrait and landscape;
  - the trip sheet gets a route view for an earlier trip, plus delete;
  - still no tabs. Whether the route view needs a second level inside the sheet is left to the design;
  - needs a Stitch design before the spec, as trip stats did.
- Data: a new on-device store for routes (positions with times) of the current trip and the last 10 trips.
- Network: the app's first network use, only to fetch map tiles over HTTPS. No account, no analytics, no upload.
- Privacy text: the location usage string says "It never leaves your device". Each tile request tells the tile server roughly where I am (which tile, plus my IP address), and routes are now stored. So the string must change.
- Docs and specs this changes:
  - `CLAUDE.md` Architecture: "no networking".
  - `specs/speedometer/spec.md`: R1 (single screen), Non-functional "No network" and "Offline", the location usage string, "location ... never stored".
  - `specs/ui-redesign/spec.md`: where the face button, status chip and mascot go in map mode.
  - `specs/trip-stats/spec.md`:
    - R9 (totals only): routes are now stored, next to the totals;
    - R14 (trip sheet): route view and delete;
    - Out of scope: "Route, map or position history" and "deleting single history entries" move into scope.
  - `intent/2026-10-26-trip-stats.md`: "No route and no coordinates" is superseded.
  - `specs/speed-accuracy/spec.md`: none. Map data still never feeds the speed estimate. The debug drive recorder is unchanged and separate.

## Constraints
- iOS 17+, SwiftUI. Apple's SwiftUI map can't show OSM tiles, so the map uses Apple's older map view, wrapped for SwiftUI. No third-party library.
- **OpenStreetMap tile policy** for `tile.openstreetmap.org` (operations.osmfoundation.org/policies/tiles, checked 2026-10-27):
  - HTTPS only, with a User-Agent that names Xpeet;
  - the credit is visible on the map, never hidden behind a toggle or under other UI;
  - tiles are cached by the server's cache headers, or for at least 7 days;
  - only tiles I'm viewing are fetched: no prefetching the road ahead, no "download area for offline";
  - the service is best effort with no guarantee, and heavy users can be blocked without notice. The tile address should be changeable without an app update.
- **Privacy:**
  - Routes stay on the phone. They are never uploaded, shared or exported.
  - They are protected while the phone is locked and kept out of device backups (proposed).
  - Deleting is final: there is no "recently deleted".
  - Tile requests are the only thing that leaves the phone.
  - No API keys in the app (REVIEW.md).
- **Driving safety:**
  - nothing on the live map needs a tap;
  - viewing history and deleting happen in the trip sheet;
  - no navigation, routing or search;
  - the speed stays the most prominent thing on screen.
- **Must not encourage speeding** (trip-stats R16): no speed colours on the map or the route, and no top speed on a route.
- Foreground only, as today (speedometer R20). The route is recorded only while the app is open, so time with the app closed shows as a gap.
- Gauge mode is unchanged, and the gauge keeps the size "199" needs.
- **Accessibility:**
  - speed and trip keep their VoiceOver labels in map mode;
  - the map itself is not read tile by tile; a route view reads the trip's totals;
  - delete works with VoiceOver (an action, not only a swipe);
  - the speed readout and the credit follow Dynamic Type;
  - the map button is at least 44 pt.
- Battery and data: drawing the map and fetching tiles cost more than the gauge alone. That cost is accepted while the map is shown. Gauge mode costs only the route recording, which uses the fixes the app already gets.

## Decisions
The owner decided on 2026-10-27:
- **Tile source:** A, which is Apple's map view with raster tiles from `tile.openstreetmap.org`. No new library and no key. Online only: offline means only recently seen tiles. Apple's logo and "Legal" link stay in a corner. The tile address is switchable, so MapLibre with OpenFreeMap can follow later.
- **Where:** a separate map mode, switched with a button.
- **Turning it on:** the map stays off until I tap the map button. The first time, a one-line note says the tiles come from OpenStreetMap, whose servers see the area I'm in.
- **Orientation:** heading-up while moving. When stopped, it keeps the last heading.
- **Trail and route history:** yes. This changes the earlier proposal: draw the trail, keep each trip's route in the history, and allow deleting it.
- **If the app is ever distributed:** it will need an App Store privacy label (likely "Coarse Location, app functionality, not linked to you") and a privacy policy that names the tile provider. Not needed while sideloaded.

The owner then accepted the route-history details below (2026-10-27):
- **How many:** routes for the same last 10 trips as the trip history.
- **Viewing:** one trip at a time, from the trip sheet. Past routes are not drawn on the live map.
- **Deleting:**
  - swipe, or the VoiceOver action, deletes one earlier trip with no extra confirmation;
  - "Delete all trips" asks once, then clears everything including the current trip.
- **Gaps:** where GPS was lost or the app was closed, the line breaks instead of joining across.
- **Storage:**
  - points are thinned to about one every 10 m, plus one at each turn;
  - points with poor GPS accuracy are skipped;
  - routes are kept out of device backups.
- **New location string:** "Xpeet uses your location to show your speed, trip totals, and your position and routes on the map. Routes stay on your device. Map tiles are downloaded from OpenStreetMap, which sees the area you are in."

## Open questions
None open.
- **Out of scope for now:**
  - turn-by-turn navigation, routing, search, addresses, places;
  - speed limits from OSM (still out, per the speedometer spec);
  - downloading map areas for offline use;
  - exporting or sharing routes (GPX and the like);
  - showing all routes at once, or heatmaps;
  - CarPlay, background recording, Live Activity.
